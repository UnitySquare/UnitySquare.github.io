FAQ

What is Unitdice?

Unitdice is an early-stage studio in Gwangju, South Korea. It develops games, AI games where AI writes the story and the choices, and AI-driven services.

What does Unitdice make?

Galaxy S25 Assistant, an AI chatbot that answers from the Galaxy S25 user manual (chat.unitdice.com); Ball Sim, a ball game in development; and services built with AI: a Gwangju delivery route comparison tool and an IT helpdesk booking system (help.unitdice.com).

Where is Unitdice based?

Gwangju, Republic of Korea.

How can I contact Unitdice?

Email contact@unitdice.com for collaborations, pilot projects, or feedback on our products.
